last_run:

## Ask
- What happened this week?
- What is coming up next week?
- Anything on your mind that did not fit?

## Steps
1. Sort what happened into done, carried over and dropped.
2. Pick the three things that matter most next week and say why.
3. Draft a one-page review in plain language.

## Approvals
- Before saving the review.

## Result
A one-page weekly review, saved to Work.
